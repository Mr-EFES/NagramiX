# NagramiX 0.3.7

NagramiX 0.3.7 corrects the final outer width of text-only broadcast-channel
posts when Wide Channel Posts is enabled.

## Targeted correction

After Telegram completes intrinsic text measurement, NagramiX expands the
outer text bubble to the maximum safe width. Short text must therefore no
longer collapse the post background to Telegram's standard compact width, and
multiline text retains the same wide outer geometry.

The correction is restricted to a single text content node. Single photos,
single videos, captions and photo/video albums keep their 0.3.6 geometry and
continue through Telegram's native media and mosaic layout paths.

## Required acceptance

1. Compare a short text-only channel post with Wide Channel Posts off and on.
2. Repeat with a multiline text-only post and reactions/metadata present.
3. Confirm that single-photo and single-video posts preserve their 0.3.6 shape.
4. Confirm that multi-photo, multi-video and mixed albums preserve their 0.3.6
   mosaic geometry.

## Validation status

Static overlay validation is required before dispatch. Native ARM64 compilation
and physical-iPhone acceptance remain pending until the authoritative
macOS/Xcode workflow completes and the resulting unsigned IPA is externally
signed and installed.
