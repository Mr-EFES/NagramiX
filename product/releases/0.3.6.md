# NagramiX 0.3.6

NagramiX 0.3.6 is based directly on the working 0.3.4 source line. The broken
0.3.5 candidate is not used as its base.

## Targeted correction

When Wide Channel Posts is enabled, an intrinsically narrow single photo or
single video now receives the same maximum safe outer width already used by
multi-photo and multi-video posts. Telegram's native media renderer continues
to own aspect fitting, playback, interaction and its existing blurred-background
treatment where the source aspect ratio does not fill the available width.

No other product behavior is deliberately changed from 0.3.4.

## Validation status

The authoritative macOS/Xcode ARM64 build, generated-source contract checks,
unsigned packaging, artifact validation and release publication passed for
exact source commit `45323da432fc37d23eba7d4ded9585defb639a9f`.
Compilation alone does not establish runtime acceptance: the published IPA
still requires external signing and physical-iPhone comparison against the
supplied single-photo/single-video reference.
